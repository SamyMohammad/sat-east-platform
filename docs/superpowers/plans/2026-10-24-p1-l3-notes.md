## Context

Students can't read topic notes yet, and `topic_assets`/`notes_opened_at` exist unused. This story adds three things:
- the in-app protected PDF viewer (watermark, no download);
- a **Notes** step that gates practice (**user**), applied only when the topic has notes;
- teacher upload from the course builder.

The teacher's book is postponed (**user**). One PDF per topic or per subtopic is the teacher's call (board Q-16), and the design supports both.

## Review focus

1. **Storage policy** on `storage.objects` (bucket `notes`, through `private.notes_object_readable(name)`):
   - an enrolled student with the topic open and the asset `ready` → can select;
   - a locked topic, no enrolment, a not-ready asset, or a path for another topic → cannot;
   - a teacher → can;
   - insert/update/delete → teacher only.
2. `mark_notes_opened(p_asset_id)`: idempotent (keeps the first timestamp); refuses a locked or un-enrolled topic or a not-ready asset (`topic_locked` / `not_enrolled` / `invalid_input`).
3. **Practice gate:** a topic with ≥ 1 ready notes asset and `notes_opened_at` null → `get_practice_questions` raises `notes_needed`, and `check_practice_answer` does too. Once opened → OK. No notes → OK, and the existing practice/quiz pgTAP still passes unchanged.
4. **Teacher RPCs:**
   - `teacher_create_notes_asset(p_topic_id, p_subtopic_id, p_title, p_page_count)` → `{asset_id, path}` (not ready);
   - `teacher_confirm_notes_asset(p_asset_id)` → it errors `invalid_input` when no `storage.objects` row exists at the path; otherwise it sets `ready`;
   - `teacher_update_notes_asset(p_asset_id, p_title, p_subtopic_id)`, `teacher_reorder_notes(p_topic_id, p_ids)` and `teacher_delete_notes_asset(p_asset_id)` (deletes the row; the client deletes the object first);
   - all of them `forbidden` for students, and audited.
5. `get_topic` gains `notes: [{asset_id, title, subtopic_title, page_count}]` (ready only, in sort order) and `notes_done` (bool). The existing keys are unchanged.
6. **Client:**
   - The watermark is on every page (`pageOverlaysBuilder`) with name · email local part · date, direction-aware.
   - Text selection is off.
   - There is no download/print/share UI.
   - The bytes are dropped on dispose.
   - The size limit comes from `settings` (the client check is UX only; the bucket limit enforces it).
