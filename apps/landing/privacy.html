Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who we are

This platform is run by Abdelrahman Elmenshawy, a mathematics teacher, to prepare students for the SAT and EST Math exams. In this policy, "we" means him. Most of our students are under 18. A parent or guardian is welcome to contact us about their child's account at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name. You may also give a phone number, country, school, grade and time zone.
  • Learning activity: the lessons you open, your answers, scores, quiz and mock results, progress and notifications.
  • Device identifiers: a random ID and basic browser or device details for each device you sign in on. We use them to enforce the limit on devices per account.
  • AI tutor conversations: if you use the AI tutor, we store your messages and its replies to review quality and fix mistakes. We do not use them to train third-party AI models.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics: technical error reports and product analytics events, collected to fix problems and improve the platform.

We do not ask for payment details on this platform during the closed beta.

Who handles data for us

We use these providers to run the service. They process data only on our behalf.

  • Supabase: database and sign-in.
  • Cloudflare: website hosting, video storage and backup storage.
  • Brevo: sending emails such as account confirmations and password resets.
  • Sentry: crash reports.
  • PostHog: product analytics.
  • An AI model provider: receives the text of a question and your message when you use the AI tutor.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Encrypted backups of the database are kept for up to 30 days, so deleted data can remain in a backup for that long before it is removed.

Deleting your data

To have your account and its data deleted, email us at the contact email (turn on JavaScript to see it) from the address on the account. If you are a parent or guardian, say which student the request is about. The teacher completes the deletion by hand. It removes the account and everything linked to it, and it cannot be undone.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: the contact email (turn on JavaScript to see it).
